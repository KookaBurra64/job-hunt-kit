# My job hunt playbook

Claude reads this file at the start of every session and updates it before the session ends.
It is how the next session knows where you are up to. You can read and edit it too.

Last updated: (date)

## Start here

One or two lines on where things stand right now, and what to do next.

## What I'm looking for

The kind of job, the level, where, remote or not, salary floor, and anything that rules a job
out. The search settings live in `my-profile.toml`; this section holds the reasons and the
things a setting can't capture ("not another call centre role", "happy to commute two days").

## Voice and writing rules

How I want my CV and letters to sound. Words I never want used. Anything I have rejected in a
draft and why, so it is not suggested again.

## What's live

The CV I'm currently sending, my LinkedIn headline and About text, if we have worked on them.
Point to the files rather than copying the text in here.

## Applications in progress

Short notes that `applications.md` doesn't hold: who I spoke to, what the interview covered,
what I promised to send. One or two lines each. Remove an entry when it is finished.

## Open items and follow-ups

Things that are half done or waiting on something, each with a date.

## Things learned

What has worked and what hasn't: job sites, recruiters, letter styles, interview questions
that came up. Keep only what will change what we do next time.

## Keeping this file current

- Update it before the session ends: "Start here", progress, new open items, anything learned.
- Remove anything that is no longer true. Don't pile new notes on top of old ones.
- Keep it under about 300 lines. Move finished applications and old notes to
  `PLAYBOOK-archive.md`.
- Never put passwords, tax file numbers, bank details or ID numbers here.
